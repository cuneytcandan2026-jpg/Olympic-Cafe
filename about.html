/ ABOUT OLYMPIC CAFE

Your local cafe in
Waltham Cross

Olympic Cafe is a warm, welcoming neighbourhood cafe serving generous British cafe classics, Mediterranean-inspired breakfasts and familiar family favourites throughout the day.

British Roots

Full English breakfasts, Sunday roasts, homemade pies and proper cafe favourites — the classics done right, every single day.

Mediterranean Character

Halloumi, feta, olives, sucuk and sun-ripened flavour woven through our breakfasts and brunch menu — a nod to the communities who make Waltham Cross home.
